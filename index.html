WEEKLY OPERATING CYCLE (MON–FRI)

MONDAY
Ingestion
Log all documents received from EGPC/ITGEST into Evidence Register. AI-01 processes new submissions. Update document status.
TUESDAY
AI Analysis
AI agents process ingested documents. AI-02/03/04/05 prepare preliminary observations, evidence gaps, exceptions, and cross-document inconsistencies. All outputs marked DRAFT.
WEDNESDAY
KABO Validation
Technical Reviewer reviews AI DRAFT observations. Rejects false positives. Validates findings. Determines severity. Requests additional evidence. Updates Findings Register. Marks validated items as VALIDATED.
THURSDAY
Client Engagement
Present validated findings to EGPC (via QA Programme Director). Log all ITGEST exchanges in writing (R-11 protocol). Document client responses in Evidence Register. Follow up on evidence gaps.
FRIDAY
Reporting
AI-06 prepares Weekly QA Report draft. QA Programme Director reviews and approves. Issue to Steering Committee. Update Dashboard RAG. Update RAID log. Archive weekly snapshot.

AI GOVERNANCE — MANDATORY CONTROLS

AI MUST NOT independently: approve architecture · close findings · accept migration results · declare readiness · approve go-live · determine contractual compliance
KABO APPROVAL REQUIRED FOR: all final findings · severity determination · all recommendations · client-facing reports · technical conclusions · deliverable acceptance · executive reporting
All AI outputs must be marked DRAFT until validated by the responsible KABO reviewer. Draft findings must never be shared with clients.
ITGEST documents: all submissions from ITGEST are self-reported evidence. AI must flag ITGEST self-assessments separately. Apply professional scepticism. Verify independently where possible.
Data security: never upload SAP credentials, passwords, security keys, unrestricted production extracts, or personal data to general AI tools. Use approved private AI platforms only.
